# GIFT opens two doors; the distribution comment mountain grows

IFSCA, the quietest of the six desks, opened both of this week's new windows — and both carry a interests-ledger flavour.

The first proposes letting research entities in GIFT-IFSC offer [model portfolios](https://ifsca.gov.in/ReportPublication/Index?MId=3qJc6ZQTmhg=) (9 November, comments due **30 November**). Baskets of securities with stated weightages are not allowed today; the clarification under the CMI Regulations, 2025 would let broker-dealers register as research entities to sell them. Per [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/ifsca-proposes-model-portfolios-in-gift-city-by-research-entities-126110900766_1.html), the proposal "follows representations from industry participants" — a textbook case of a rulebook being drafted in response to an industry ask, which is exactly what this register exists to log.

The second proposes [guidelines for engaging Financial Solicitation Professionals](https://ifsca.gov.in/ReportPublication/Index?MId=2W3lMrHg9rU=) (9 November, comments due **30 November**): a disclosure-based regime, with a public FSP list, for outside individuals soliciting on behalf of regulated financial institutions. Comment publication is unspecified in both papers.

At IRDAI, the already-closed [distribution consultation](https://lobbywatch.cashlessconsumer.in/consultations.html) keeps scaling. [ET Now reported](https://www.etnownews.com/news/irdai-gets-over-2-lakh-responses-on-distribution-reforms-draft-rules-likely-by-middecember-article-156503265) (13 November) that it has received 2 lakh-plus comments from around 15,000 stakeholders, with draft rules targeted for mid-December — and that chairman Ajay Seth met broker bodies lobbying on commission parity and who pays for technology-platform costs. Ledger updated with the reported volume. Note what the reporting is *not*: minutes, attendee lists or comment disclosures. This remains the standing first candidate for the [RTI comments template](https://lobbywatch.cashlessconsumer.in/access.html), which is still at zero filings.

SEBI's week: the [MII related-activities window](https://www.sebi.gov.in/reports-and-statistics/reports/oct-2026/consultation-paper-on-review-of-regulation-38-2-of-the-secc-regulations-2018-and-regulation-7-c-i-of-the-dandp-regulations-2018-for-providing-an-automatic-approval-route-for-investment-in-related-a-_105391.html) closed on schedule (12 November, ledger flipped to `closed`). Post-close, the Bombay Chartered Accountants' Society went on record on the issue-proceeds paper — backing mandatory monitoring agencies for all issues and CA firms with peer-review certificates as eligible ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/bcas-urges-sebi-to-allow-ca-firms-to-monitor-issue-proceeds-of-companies-126111301243_1.html), 13 November) — a reminder that commenters self-publish when regulators won't. And SEBI chief Tuhin Kanta Pandey said AI-usage guidelines for capital markets are coming, with disclosure and human-in-the-loop guardrails ([PTI via ET](http://legal.economictimes.indiatimes.com/amp/news/regulators/sebi-to-issue-ai-usage-guidelines-for-capital-markets-focus-on-investor-protection-pandey/135857713), 15 November) — the outcome of the [June 2025 AI/ML consultation](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jun-2025/1750415065695.pdf), now added to the ledger as `sebi-aiml-2025`.

Still flagged: rechecked 16 November, IRDAI's own [exposure-drafts listing](https://irdai.gov.in/exposure-drafts) still does not carry the 3 November subsidiary-instructions draft — its newest entries remain the 31 July statements of response. Watch it; the window closes **23 November**. Live countdowns: RBI PSL consolidation due **26 November**, SEBI AML/CFT certification **27 November**, both IFSCA papers **30 November**.
