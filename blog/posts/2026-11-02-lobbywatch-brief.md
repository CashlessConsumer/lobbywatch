# The IRDAI window shut. Now the count becomes a question

One closing in the [consultation ledger](https://lobbywatch.cashlessconsumer.in/consultations.html) this week: IRDAI's paper on recalibrating the economics of insurance distribution shut on **31 October**, after the regulator's [24 October release](https://irdai.gov.in/document-detail?documentId=9944637) reported more than **650 comments** and extended the deadline from 25 October. The row still carries `comments_published: unknown`. Feedback flowed through an IIB-run portal, an Excel template and an email inbox — channels that produce a count, not a public record of who asked for what. IRDAI's own June draft framework for regulation-making, still unnotified (`proposed` in the ledger), would require a published statement of response by notification date. The test for this file is whether that standard arrives before the final rules do.

The count also has a named shape. [ETBFSI reported](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/banks-sfbs-raise-irdai-insurance-commission-concerns-through-iba-asfbi-seek-transition-time/135245029) banks and small finance banks routing objections through the **Indian Banks' Association** and the Association of Small Finance Banks, seeking transition time — one mid-sized private bank CEO said its representation went via IBA. IBA is a registered interest on LobbyWatch's [interests page](https://lobbywatch.cashlessconsumer.in/interests.html), and bancassurance fee income is exactly what the proposed caps on credit-linked insurance would cut.

Two SEBI windows close this week, both in the ledger with comment publication unverified: issue-proceeds monitoring on **3 November** ([paper](https://www.sebi.gov.in/reports-and-statistics/reports/oct-2026/consultation-paper-on-recommendations-of-the-working-group-for-review-of-sebi-lodr-regulations-2015-review-of-framework-for-monitoring-and-disclosure-of-utilization-of-issue-proceeds_105242.html)) and SME IPO rules on **4 November** ([paper](https://www.sebi.gov.in/reports-and-statistics/reports/oct-2026/consultation-paper-on-recommendations-pursuant-to-review-of-provisions-of-sebi-issue-of-capital-and-disclosure-requirements-regulations-2018-related-to-initial-public-offer-by-small-and-medium-ente-_105252.html)). The MII automatic-approval-route paper runs to **12 November**.

The [doors ledger](https://lobbywatch.cashlessconsumer.in/doors.html) records no appointment dated this week. Fresh reporting brushes against it: a [30 October Businessworld investigation](https://www.tradingview.com/news/moodys:de435d460fd4e:0-lic-director-sat-on-fairfax-co-s-board-while-lic-was-selling-idbi-to-fairfax/) maps 17 former public officials across Fairfax group companies — including two moves already in our ledger (B.P. Kanungo to IIFL Finance's chair; G. Mahalingam to LIC's board). The article itself states there is no allegation and no public evidence that any of them influenced the IDBI sale; we say the same. Its overlap of record — Mahalingam's 19 December 2025 Thomas Cook India seat, held while he sat on LIC's board and investment committee — is a candidate row, not a recorded one: LobbyWatch adds door moves only off primary sources, and the Thomas Cook and CSB Bank appointments await exactly that.

With the IRDAI window shut, the ready next step is ours: the comments-disclosure RTI template (TPL-01) against IRDAI's CPIO. The [RTI log](https://lobbywatch.cashlessconsumer.in/access.html) holds two template-ready entries and zero filings. Next watch: the two SEBI deadlines, and whether IRDAI's 650+ comments ever become visible.
