GIFT opens two doors; the distribution comment mountain grows

IFSCA, the quietest of the six desks, opened both of this week's new windows — and both carry a interests-ledger flavour.

The first proposes letting research entities in GIFT-IFSC offer model portfolios (9 November, comments due 30 November). Baskets of securities with stated weightages are not allowed today; the clarification under the CMI Regulations, 2025 would let broker-dealers register as research entities to sell them. Per Business Standard, the proposal "follows representations from industry participants" — a textbook case of a rulebook being drafted in response to an industry ask, which is exactly what this register exists to log.

The second proposes guidelines for engaging Financial Solicitation Professionals (9 November, comments due 30 November): a disclosure-based regime, with a public FSP list, for outside individuals soliciting on behalf of regulated financial institutions. Comment publication is unspecified in both papers.

At IRDAI, the already-closed distribution consultation keeps scaling. ET Now reported (13 November) that it has received 2 lakh-plus comments from around 15,000 stakeholders, with draft rules targeted for mid-December — and that chairman Ajay Seth met broker bodies lobbying on commission parity and who pays for technology-platform costs. Ledger updated with the reported volume. Note what the reporting is not: minutes, attendee lists or comment disclosures. This remains the standing first candidate for the RTI comments template, which is still at zero filings.

SEBI's week: the MII related-activities window closed on schedule (12 November, ledger flipped to closed). Post-close, the Bombay Chartered Accountants' Society went on record on the issue-proceeds paper — backing mandatory monitoring agencies for all issues and CA firms with peer-review certificates as eligible (Business Standard, 13 November) — a reminder that commenters self-publish when regulators won't. And SEBI chief Tuhin Kanta Pandey said AI-usage guidelines for capital markets are coming, with disclosure and human-in-the-loop guardrails (PTI via ET, 15 November) — the outcome of the June 2025 AI/ML consultation, now added to the ledger as sebi-aiml-2025.

Still flagged: rechecked 16 November, IRDAI's own exposure-drafts listing still does not carry the 3 November subsidiary-instructions draft — its newest entries remain the 31 July statements of response. Watch it; the window closes 23 November. Live countdowns: RBI PSL consolidation due 26 November, SEBI AML/CFT certification 27 November, both IFSCA papers 30 November.
